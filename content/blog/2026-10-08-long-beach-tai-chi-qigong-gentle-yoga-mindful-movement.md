Not everyone calms down by sitting still. If your mind settles better when your body is moving slowly, Long Beach has a quiet network of low-cost options: city park classes in tai chi, qigong and gentle yoga, plus a weekly meditation sit for when you do want stillness. This guide is a companion to our broader [mental health and mindfulness resources guide](/blog/2026-08-20-mental-health-mindfulness-resources-long-beach), and it focuses on the movement side. Everything below is a public program with a listing we checked, but schedules and registration change, so confirm before you go.

## Tai Chi & Qigong at Bixby Park (Alamitos Beach / Bluff Park)

The City of Long Beach Parks, Recreation and Marine department lists a **Tai Chi & Qigong** class at **Bixby Park** (130 Cherry Ave, Social Hall #1), taught by instructor Howard Richner. The class runs in four-week blocks on Saturdays: October 3-24 and November 7-28, 2026. The listing does not show a start time or a price. The October block shows registration closed, and the November block says to see the price at registration, so check the [November listing](https://www.active.com/long-beach-ca/tai-chi/classes/tai-chi-and-qigong-2026-110285664) for current availability. The listings show an age range that looks like a typo ("18-255"), so confirm eligibility when you register.

Bixby Park is also home to a Saturday farmers market and sits on the bluff above the beach path, so a morning class pairs easily with a walk along the water afterward.

## Mindfulness Tai Chi & Qigong at El Dorado Park West (East Long Beach)

On the inland side, a **Mindfulness Tai Chi & Qigong** class meets on Wednesdays in the Activity Room at **El Dorado Park West** (2800 N. Studebaker Rd), taught by Richard Harvey. The current block runs October 7-28, 2026. As with the Bixby class, the [listing](https://www.active.com/long-beach-ca/tai-chi/classes/mindfulness-tai-chi-and-qigong-2026-110335838) shows no time or price and shows registration closed for this block. Parks classes of this kind tend to repeat in later blocks (the Bixby class has sessions listed through November), so it's worth checking back.

Tai chi and qigong are low-impact, so they suit people who find high-intensity workouts stressful or who are returning to movement after a long break. The slow pace and the attention on breath are what make them "mindful" rather than just gentle.

## Gentle Yoga at Recreation Park (Park Estates / East Long Beach)

**Gentle Yoga - All Levels** meets on Mondays, October 5-26, 2026, in the Social Hall at **Recreation Park** (4900 E. 7th St), with instructor Silvana Behrens. The [listing](https://www.active.com/long-beach-ca/yoga/classes/gentle-yoga-all-levels-2026-110286096) mentions a walk-in fee and a discount but gives no amounts or class time, and registration is shown as closed for this block. For a free alternative that needs no registration, the daily Yoga on the Bluff class above Junipero Beach is covered in our [free and outdoor wellness guide](/blog/2026-08-24-free-low-cost-wellness-long-beach).

If gentle movement is part of managing a specific condition, such as chronic pain or stress-related tension, individualized [yoga therapy](/yoga-therapy) is a different thing from a group class: it is a one-to-one practice designed around your needs.

## A Weekly Meditation Sit in Belmont Heights

For stillness, **InsightLA** lists a **Long Beach Sunday Practice Group** at the Sacred Roots Healing Center (2841 E Broadway, Belmont Heights). It is a hybrid session, so you can attend in person or online, on Sundays from 10:00 to 11:30 a.m. Dates listed include October 11, 18 and 25 and continue through the end of December. Registration is required, and the listing does not state a cost, so check the [InsightLA Long Beach page](https://insightla.org/location/long-beach-sacred-roots-healing-center/) before you go. The listed teachers are Cayce Howe, David Le and Wendy Block.

## How to Choose

A few practical points help when you're comparing options. If you want something free and drop-in, look at the outdoor bluff class rather than the registered parks blocks. If you want a structured series with the same people each week, the four-week parks classes give you that rhythm. If your goal is to learn a seated meditation practice, the Sunday group is the closest fit. And if you're in a hard season and want more than a class, our [mental health resources guide](/blog/2026-08-20-mental-health-mindfulness-resources-long-beach) lists therapists, support groups and crisis lines. For anyone in immediate distress, call or text 988 any time.
