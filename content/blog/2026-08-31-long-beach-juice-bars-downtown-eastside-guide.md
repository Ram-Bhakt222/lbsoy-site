## Filling in the Map Beyond Belmont Shore

Our first Long Beach juice bar guide back in June covered the Belmont Shore and Bluff Heights cluster, and those spots — Salud Juice, Pressed Juicery, Tru Nature — haven't changed. What has changed is everything east and inland of them. This update covers Downtown, California Heights, and the CSULB corridor, and includes an honest correction: a longtime East Village spot has closed since our last check. Consider this the second half of the map.

## Downtown Long Beach: Movita Takes the Broadway Corridor

**Movita Juice Bar** (421 W Broadway, Ste 461) is the clearest Downtown pick, open daily from as early as 6 a.m. on weekdays through 8:30 p.m. Everything is built on fresh fruit, organic açaí, in-house almond milk, and all-natural protein, with the juice and smoothie menus rotating seasonally. It sits close enough to the Promenade and the Downtown Long Beach Alliance's business corridor that it works as a stop before or after a walk through the East Village Arts District.

Here's the update worth knowing if you've been going to the East Village for juice: **Revive Juice Bar**, the longtime spot at 145 Linden Ave, has closed. It was a neighborhood fixture for years, and if you're used to routing there, Movita's Broadway location is the closest still-open replacement — about a ten-minute walk. We'll keep watching for whatever opens in that Linden Ave space next and update this guide when something does.

## California Heights: A Second Movita Location

Movita isn't just a Downtown brand — the company runs a second Long Beach location at **2288 E Carson St** in **California Heights**, open daily 7 a.m.–8 p.m. Same fresh, no-added-sugar approach to juice and smoothies, and it's a useful anchor if you're coming from Bixby Knolls or the Cal Heights side of the 405, since it's a few minutes closer than doubling back to 2nd Street.

## Near CSULB: Juice It Up! on Spring Street

Out toward **Cal State Long Beach**, **Juice It Up!** (6508 E Spring St, across from Millikan High School) opened as a locally owned franchise run by a Long Beach resident. The menu covers handcrafted smoothies, raw juices, and superfruit bowls, and it's become a go-to for students and staff on the university side of the city who don't want to cross town for a smoothie. It's a chain concept, but the ownership is local, and the Spring Street Shopping Center location makes it the most convenient healthy stop in that stretch of East Long Beach.

## The PCH / Los Altos Corridor: Robeks

Further along Pacific Coast Highway near the **Los Altos** area, **Robeks** (4547 E PCH) has quietly been one of the more consistent juice-and-smoothie options on the east side, open seven days a week (Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–7 p.m., weekends 9 a.m.–7 p.m.). Between Robeks, Nekter, and Tru Bowl (all covered in our original guide), the PCH corridor from the Marketplace out to Los Altos now has more healthy grab-and-go options than almost any other stretch of the city.

## How This Fits Into a Healthy Long Beach Routine

The pattern that works best here isn't picking one "best" juice bar — it's knowing which one is on your actual route. If you're Downtown for a morning at the Aquarium or a walk through the East Village Arts District, Movita on Broadway is on the way. If you're dropping kids at Millikan or heading to CSULB, Juice It Up! is closer than driving to the water. And if California Heights or Bixby Knolls is home base, the Carson Street Movita saves the trip to 2nd Street entirely.

None of these require a special trip. Pair Downtown Movita with a loop through the East Village Arts District's galleries and coffee shops on a Saturday morning. Pair the Spring Street Juice It Up! with a lap of the nearby CSULB campus paths or a stop at the Farmers Marketplace at Clark & Spring on a Sunday. Pair the Carson Street Movita with a walk through California Heights' tree-lined blocks — one of the more underrated flat walking neighborhoods in the city. The businesses come and go (see: Revive), but the habit of building a short walk around a healthy stop is the part that actually sticks.
