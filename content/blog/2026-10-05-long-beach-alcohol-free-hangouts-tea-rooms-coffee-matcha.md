Our [August guide to Long Beach mocktails](/blog/2026-08-15-sober-curious-mocktails-healthy-nightlife-long-beach) covered the bars that take zero-proof drinks seriously. This is the other half of the sober-curious question: places where nobody expects you to order a drink at all. Long Beach has a quiet, growing set of tea rooms, matcha counters and coffee shops that work as genuine third places, somewhere to sit for two hours with friends and walk home clear-headed.

One honest note first. Few of these spots advertise themselves as "sober" venues, and some serve alcohol alongside tea or coffee. We have flagged which ones do, so you can choose the room that fits your night.

## Wrigley and Downtown Long Beach: Tea, Listening Rooms and Community Coffee

**Twelfth House Tea Sanctuary** (1950 W Willow St) is the closest thing in the city to a dedicated tea-house evening. When it was announced in 2023, co-owner Jasmine Thong described it as a "third place" and an alcohol-free gathering spot, with reservations, no time limit and an unlimited special-blend tea format. Its website currently lists evening events such as a life drawing session and a phone-free "Sonata Lounge" listening night. It also lists a "cocktails and cinema" night, so check the event before you book if staying alcohol-free matters to you. Visit Long Beach includes it among the city's tea rooms, and it frames the place around mindfulness and creative gatherings.

**Dedo Coffee** (2156 Pacific Ave, Wrigley) is a small shop built around a hi-fi sound system, where the owners play vinyl from their own collection. The menu is deliberately short, just a few coffees, teas and rotating kombuchas with pastries from Colossus Bread, and it hosts occasional listening sessions. It is an easy way to spend an evening with music and no bar in sight; check its Instagram for session dates, since we could not confirm a standing schedule.

**Wrigley Coffee** (437 W Willow St) is a social-enterprise cafe. It serves Common Room Roasters coffee and Homeboy Bakery pastries, and runs trauma-informed workforce training that reinvests profits into Forward Endeavors, so a latte here also funds local job programs. It opened in early 2022 in the old Fox Coffee House space and was still operating as of August 2026.

**The Wicked Wolf** (2332 Pacific Ave) is the hybrid. Visit Long Beach describes a Victorian-inspired tea lounge by day and cocktail bar by night, with specialty teas, matcha and scones at weekend brunch. Its non-alcoholic menu, including the Fountain of Youth with lime, cucumber and butterfly pea tea, is listed in Visit Long Beach's mocktail guide. It is a good daytime option; remember that it is a cocktail bar after dark.

## Belmont Shore and Alamitos Beach: Matcha and a Slow Cup

**Stereoscope** (4925 E 2nd St) in **Belmont Shore** opened in 2022 and is singled out in Long Beachize's July 2026 coffee roundup for exceptional matcha. It is the easiest stop to add to a walk along 2nd Street, and it fits neatly after the free Saturday morning [Tour the Shore walking group](/blog/2026-09-01-long-beach-run-walk-clubs-bixby-knolls-signal-hill).

**Black Dog Coffee Roasters** (1101 E 3rd St, **Alamitos Beach**) opened in late 2023 and serves medium-to-dark roasts a few blocks from the sand. Pair it with a morning at Yoga on the Bluff, which meets daily at 11 a.m. on the grass at Ocean Blvd. and Junipero Ave., or the 6 p.m. weekday sunset sessions that run through the end of October.

## Afternoon Tea, East Village Style

**Chez Bacchus** (743 E 4th St) runs afternoon tea service on Saturdays and Sundays at 11:30 a.m. and 2 p.m. Long Beachize (August 2026) reports $65 per person for the Bacchus Tower and $115 for the Queen's Tower, which adds Veuve Clicquot Rich Rosé champagne service. Tables are set with mismatched china and loose-leaf options such as rose petal, Earl Grey and ginger-peach oolong. If you are skipping alcohol, the $65 tower is the one to book; ask when you reserve how the tea is served so you know exactly what is included.

## A Standing Alcohol-Free Social Calendar

If the goal is meeting people rather than a place, Meetup's Long Beach sober listings show the **Dry Spell Social Club** hosting an alcohol-free "Pumpkins and Karaoke" evening on Saturday, October 17 at 7 p.m. The listing does not state a venue or price, so open the RSVP page first. We could not verify a regular Long Beach schedule for the group beyond that date.

## How to Pick the Right Room

Match the room to the night. For conversation with a group of friends, a reservation-based tea sanctuary or a Chez Bacchus table gives you a defined block of time. For solo reading or work, Stereoscope and Wrigley Coffee are the low-pressure choices. For music, Dedo's listening sessions are the draw. For the days after a late night, a bluff walk and a matcha is a perfectly good plan.

None of this is medical or recovery advice. If your relationship with alcohol or stress is something you want structured support for, that is a conversation for a licensed clinician. If you are building a broader routine around sleep, stress and energy, a credentialed [yoga therapist](/yoga-therapy) can help design a plan around your own health goals.
